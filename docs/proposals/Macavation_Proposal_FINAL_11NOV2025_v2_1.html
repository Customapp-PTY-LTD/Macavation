Executive Summary

Project Overview

This proposal outlines a comprehensive digital transformation initiative for Macavation (Pty) Ltd to modernize and streamline macadamia kernel and oil production operations. The proposed Admin Portal will replace manual, spreadsheet-based processes with an integrated, real-time management system that enhances operational efficiency, ensures food safety compliance, provides actionable business intelligence, AND includes a comprehensive Sales CRM for customer relationship management and sales pipeline tracking.

Business Challenge

Macavation currently manages complex production operations across nut and oil divisions using manual processes, disconnected Excel spreadsheets, and paper-based quality control forms. This creates:

  • Data duplication and errors: Manual entry into multiple spreadsheets causes inconsistencies and time waste
  • Limited real-time visibility: Management cannot see current stock, production status, or sales performance instantly
  • Difficult traceability: Hard to track batches from grower receipt through processing to customer delivery
  • Compliance risks: Paper-based quality controls make audits difficult and increase food safety risks
  • Fragmented sales process: Customer data, quotations, and sales pipeline tracked in disconnected tools
  • Delayed financial insights: Cost accounting and profitability analysis happens days or weeks after production

The Macavation Solution

A unified, role-based admin portal that digitizes every operational touchpoint from grower intake through production, quality control, stock management, sales CRM, to customer delivery—providing real-time visibility, ensuring compliance, and enabling data-driven decision making.

Current State Analysis

📊 Manual Data Management

Currently using 5+ disconnected Excel files including "Macadamia Kernel Stock on Hand '25", "Oil SOH and Production Figures", "Supplier and Customer Database", "Kernel Statistics", and "Sales Forecast". Each file requires manual updates, creating opportunities for errors and version control issues.

📝 Paper-Based QA Processes

Quality control uses paper forms (MP0217 sample submissions, GMP checklists MP02-35, testing checklists) that must be manually transcribed into digital records. This delays decision-making and makes audit preparation time-consuming.

⏱️ Delayed Cost Accounting

Production costs are calculated days after batches are completed because raw material usage is recorded on paper job cards, then manually entered into Excel, then calculated. This prevents real-time profitability analysis.

📞 Fragmented Customer Management

Customer database, sales pipeline, quotations, and follow-ups are managed across spreadsheets, email, and manual tracking. Peter (Sales Executive) lacks a unified view of opportunities, customer history, and sales performance.

🔍 Limited Traceability

Tracking a specific batch from grower delivery through processing stages to final customer shipment requires searching multiple paper documents and Excel files—time-consuming and error-prone during recalls or audits.

📈 No Real-Time Dashboards

Management cannot see current production status, stock levels by style/location, or sales performance without requesting reports. This delays decision-making and prevents proactive management.

Proposed Solution: Macavation Admin Portal

🎯 Role-Based Portal

Customized dashboards for each team member (Mark, Peter, Simone, Josslyn, Brandon) showing only relevant information and functions for their role—reducing clutter and improving usability.

📱 Digital Data Capture

Replace all paper forms and manual spreadsheet entry with digital forms accessible via tablets, smartphones, laptops, or desktop—capturing data once at the source with QR code/barcode support.

🔄 Real-Time Integration

API integration with Palladium ERP to exchange raw material receipts and production data—eliminating duplicate entry while maintaining system independence for specialized macadamia operations.

🤝 Comprehensive Sales CRM

Full-featured customer relationship management with lead tracking, opportunity pipeline, quotation generation, customer profiles, sales forecasting, and automated invoicing—all integrated with inventory and production data.

🔬 Digital Quality Management

Electronic sample submission (MP0217), automated test result calculations, GMP digital checklists, and complete audit trail for food safety compliance (HACCP, R638, R962).

📊 Advanced Analytics

Real-time dashboards with production trends (YoY, MoM), cost per kilogram analysis, recovery rate tracking, sales performance vs forecast, and custom report generation with visual graphs.

Detailed Workflow Process

Kernel Production Workflow (17 Steps)

1

Grower Delivery & Sample Submission

Grower delivers NIS (Nuts in Shell) to facility. Simone (QA) or team member submits digital sample using tablet/phone (replaces paper MP0217 form). System captures: supplier name, delivery date, batch number, wet weight, moisture content. Batch automatically assigned unique tracking number.

2

Laboratory Testing & Analysis

Simone conducts sample crack-out, float test, unsound kernel breakdown. All test data entered digitally with automatic calculations: recovery percentage, unsound percentage, float percentage, defect breakdown. System flags quality issues (mould, germination, stinkbug) automatically.

3

Main Run Document Generation

Once sample testing complete, Josslyn generates digital Main Run Document showing estimated payment based on test results and recovery rate. Document sent to grower via email/SMS with batch details and estimated payout.

4

Approval & Raw Material Intake

The General Manager or authorized person reviews test results and approves or rejects batch. If approved, Josslyn processes raw material intake digitally, automatically updating stock on hand. Physical batch moved to production queue.

5

Production Planning

Mark (Production Manager) creates digital production plan based on: current stock levels, customer orders, equipment availability. System suggests batch scheduling and resource allocation.

6

Cracking Stage

Production team starts batch cracking. Digital job card opened on tablet showing batch details. Team records: start time, quantity in (NIS), cracking duration, wholes, uncracks. System calculates minute test averages automatically.

7

GMP Quality Checks (Cracking)

Simone or QA team performs digital GMP checklist (MP02-35) during cracking: cleaning verification, peracetic acid sanitizer test, metal detector checks, scale verification, temperature monitoring. All checks timestamped with user signature.

8

Washing Stage

Batch moves to washing. Digital recording of: crates in, floater crates out, sinker crates out, floater quantity, sinker quantity, peracitic acid test results. Waste (salt & pepper, shell fines, compost) automatically calculated and recorded.

9

Sorting Stage

Kernel sorted into styles (SP, 0, 1, 1S, 4L, 5, 6, 7/8 butter, etc). Digital recording of quantities by style with automatic reconciliation against washing output. Oil waste and compost quantities tracked for cost accounting.

10

Drying

Sorted kernel moved to drying rooms/silos. System tracks: drying temperature, moisture checks (target <2%), drying duration. Mark logs equipment performance and any maintenance issues.

11

GMP Quality Checks (Packing)

Before packing, Simone performs digital GMP checks: footbath sanitizer, UV lamp operation, cold container temps, gas flush checks, metal detector verification with official test samples, weight scale verification.

12

Packing & Carton Recording

Kernel packed into cartons (by style: SP, 0, 1, 1S, 4L, 5, 6 and by carton size). Digital recording of carton count and weights per style. System generates packing summary showing total weight by style and grade.

13

Quality Testing (Final Batch)

Simone submits samples to external lab (or conducts in-house testing) for: moisture, FFA, PV, E.coli, Salmonella, Listeria, Aflatoxin. Lab results uploaded and linked to batch in system. System alerts if any results fail specifications.

14

Positive Release & Transfer to Packed Stock

Once all quality tests pass, Simone (or authorized QA person) digitally approves positive release. Josslyn creates digital transfer sheet moving batch from "Production" to "Packed Stock on Hand". Stock levels automatically updated by style and location.

15

Batch Results & Grower Payment

Josslyn sends final batch results document to grower showing: actual recovery rate, kernel breakdown by style and grade, payment calculation. Grower invoicing requested through system. Payment tracked in creditors module.

16

Customer Order Fulfillment

Peter (Sales) receives customer order. System checks stock availability by style. If available, Peter creates sales order which reserves stock. Dispatch team prepares shipment with digital dispatch checklist. Certificate of Analysis (COA) automatically generated with batch traceability.

17

Dispatch & Invoicing

Shipment dispatched with visual inspection documented digitally. Stock automatically reduced from packed SOH. Peter or Josslyn generates customer invoice directly from system. Customer receives invoice via email with payment tracking in system.

Oil Production Workflow (11 Steps)

1

Raw Material Procurement

Brandon (Oil Plant) or the General Manager identify raw material needs: crude kernel dust, food grade kernel, shell/crush. Digital purchase order created. Josslyn tracks creditor payment and raw material receipt. Stock automatically updated.

2

Production Planning (Oil)

Mark or Brandon create daily oil production plan: select product type (crude oil, extra virgin oil, protein powder, cake), specify raw material mix formula, allocate equipment and shift resources.

3

Raw Material Usage Recording

Brandon records raw material consumption digitally on tablet (replaces paper job cards): quantity of kernel dust used, food grade kernel used, shell/crush used. Entered per production run (day/night shift).

4

Production Mix & Processing

Digital mix formula guides production process. Brandon records production parameters: temperatures, pressing duration, batch identification. System tracks equipment runtime and downtime for maintenance scheduling.

5

Finished Goods Recording

Brandon records finished goods output: crude oil quantity (liters/kg), extra virgin oil quantity, protein powder quantity, cake quantity. System calculates oil recovery rate and cost per kilogram automatically.

6

GMP Quality Checks (Oil)

Simone performs digital GMP checklist for oil production: receiving checks, FFA tests during processing, visual inspection for contamination, production sheet verification, gas flush checks, metal detector tests, moisture tests.

7

Oil Packing

Oil products packed into containers (drums, bottles, IBCs). Digital recording of container count, fill weights, batch codes. System tracks packing materials usage.
