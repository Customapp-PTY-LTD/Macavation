3. Phase 2 plan re-checked against the actual system Estimate revised

We reviewed the Phase 2 implementation plan line by line against the system as it stands today. A substantial amount of work the plan lists as outstanding has, in fact, already been built and is in the system — including dashboard measures, stock alert handling, WhatsApp message sending, shell lot automation, mass balance and procurement variance.

The remaining development effort is materially smaller than the plan states: roughly 10 to 15 days rather than 58 to 72. The plan document is being updated to match reality, so that planning and costing from here are accurate.

4. Permissions review, ahead of the workshop Needs a decision

We examined how access control is actually working in the live UAT system, in preparation for the role permissions workshop.

The portal controls access in two separate places: which buttons a person can see on screen, and what the system will actually carry out when asked. These two are currently out of step with each other.

Layer What it does Today
The buttons Decides which buttons a person can see. Six of the eight roles see only 2 of the 25 controlled buttons.
The engine Decides what the system carries out when asked. Every role is permitted 186 or more operations.

Those permitted operations include adjusting stock on hand, approving job cards, completing batches and creating dispatch orders. Only user administration is correctly restricted today.

Hiding a button does not prevent the operation behind it. Closing this gap requires one business decision — who should be able to do what — which is exactly what the workshop is designed to produce. No development can begin on it until that decision exists.

5. Workshop pack prepared Ready to circulate

A six-page guide for the permissions workshop is ready: what is being decided, what is true today, a 25-action decision grid to complete during the session, five questions needing answers, a 90-minute agenda and a sign-off sheet.
