The portal, in WhatsApp.

The messaging line is already built and connected — it just has nothing to say yet. Text it today and it can enrol you, greet you by name, and offer three words: help, yes and no. Here are the four things worth putting behind it, drawn as real conversations. Two are built and on their way to the dev site; two are waiting on a decision.

The mechanism

Who sees what is already decided for you

A phone number only gets answered once it has been linked to a real portal user, and that link brings the person’s role with it. Every command is then checked against that role before it runs. So the same word, texted from two different phones, gets two different answers — and the help menu only ever lists what that person is allowed to do.

Before anyone signs this off

Four things that shape what is possible

None of these are reasons not to do it. They are the reasons the four features look the way they do above.

Needs setting up

Nothing runs on a timer yet

Several parts of the system carry notes describing daily schedules, but those schedules were never actually created. The daily digest is not going out. Anything automatic, the stock alerts included, needs that put in place first.

Meta approval

You cannot message someone cold

WhatsApp only allows a business to start a conversation using wording Meta has approved in advance. Replies within a day of the person texting are free-form. So the alert in 03 needs its wording submitted and approved before it can ever send.

Design constraint

There is no link back into the portal

No screen in the portal can be opened directly by web address — every link lands on the home page. So a WhatsApp feature has to finish the job in the chat. That is why every mockup above answers in full rather than saying “open the portal to see more”.

Not yet possible

Photographs will not work today

Pictures sent in cannot be downloaded and kept — the key to fetch them sits with our messaging provider, not with us. So photographing a delivery note or a quality defect is off the table until that is arranged. Worth knowing before it gets promised.

Recommendation

Two are built. Two need a decision.

Latest report and closing an alert from your phone are written, tested and waiting for review. Neither needed a database change — every permission and lookup they use already existed and simply had never been called.

What is still open: where the per-style kernel figures should be read from, and whether the unprompted alert goes to one person or a group. Capturing a delivery by text has been dropped — there is nowhere in the system to save one.
