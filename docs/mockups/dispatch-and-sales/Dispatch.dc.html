How a dispatch moves through the 5 steps

1 · Dispatch created

Pete or Mike uses Send to Dispatch: customer, collection date, cartons per batch. Thando gets a "Pick a dispatch" task.

2 · Stock picked

Thando checks and confirms what she picked. If she changes anything, Joslyn, Mike and Pete are told what changed.changes tell 3 people

3 · Pricing confirmed

Prices fill in from the customer's price list. Pete can change any price before confirming.new

4 · Truck loaded

When the truck arrives: truck details and checks (the form that is there today).new step

5 · Invoice sent

Joslyn gets a task to send the invoice and documents, and ticks it off when done.new step
