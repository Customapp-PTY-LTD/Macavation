How Macavation works — at a glance

Macavation follows two product streams that share a common set of support modules. Material flows left to right — from intake through production and stock into dispatch. Click any box to jump to that section.

Getting started

Signing in, finding your way around, and understanding what you can see.

Signing in & navigating

Everything starts with your browser. Open the Macavation address your organisation gave you (bookmark it), sign in, and you're ready to go.

Signing in

  1. Open the Macavation URL in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox (latest version).
  2. Sign in with the method your admin set up — usually Google or email and password.
  3. If it fails, check caps lock, try a password reset, or ask your system administrator.

Your daily view

Dashboards, My Day, and at-a-glance summaries to start your shift.

Dashboard

The dashboard is your landing page — a snapshot of today's numbers. What you see depends on your role: some users get production stats, others get KPI cards or task lists.

Think of the dashboard as a daily health check. Glance at the cards, then dive into the modules that need attention. If the numbers look stale, hit refresh in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Open Dashboard from the sidebar (or land here automatically after sign-in).
  2. Read the summary cards — users, production batches, packing, tasks — for a quick snapshot.
  3. Click any card or link to jump into the relevant module for detail.
  4. Use the sidebar for everything else.

My Day

My Day is your personal to-do list — it shows what needs your attention based on your role. Approvals waiting, batches to check, tasks to complete.

Work through items top to bottom. Each one links straight to the module where you'll do the actual work. Completing tasks there updates everyone else's dashboards automatically.

How to use it

  1. Click the My Day icon in the top bar, or open it from the sidebar.
  2. Scan your task list — items are sorted by priority or time.
  3. Click a task to jump to the right module (intake, production, dispatch, etc.).
  4. Complete the work there, and My Day updates automatically.

Material journey dashboard

This dashboard shows you where material is right now — from intake through production and into the warehouse. Think of it as a bird's-eye view of everything in motion.

Best for roles that watch movement across sites or stages (operations managers, integrators). Each card links to the module that owns that step.

How to use it

  1. Open Material journey from the dashboard or sidebar.
  2. Click Refresh to make sure you're seeing live data.
  3. Scan the cards for exceptions or batches that need action.
  4. Click a card to jump into the owning module and take action there.

Executive dashboard

Built for leadership — KPIs, charts, and export-ready reports. Choose which widgets to show and generate PDFs when you need them for meetings.

How to use it

  1. Open Executive dashboard from the sidebar.
  2. Click Customize to choose which KPI widgets and charts appear.
  3. Use Generate report for a PDF you can share.
  4. For line-level detail, drill into Kernel or Oil & protein modules — these numbers are summaries.

CRM — Contact database

CRM is your single source of truth for people and organisations. Growers, suppliers, customers, oil processors — they're all here, organised into tabs.

Keep contacts accurate because other modules (intake, dispatch, invoicing) pull names and details from here. A wrong email in CRM means a wrong email on dispatch paperwork.

  1. Open CRM from the sidebar and pick the right tab for the contact type.

Add / edit contact (dialog)

Opens when you click Add Contact or edit a row. Fill in identity, phone, email, and address fields — then save. Downstream modules pick up the contact immediately.

  1. Confirm the contact type (NIS supplier, oil customer, etc.) so the record lands in the right tab.
  2. Complete identity and address fields as dispatch and finance teams expect them.
  3. Save — the grid refreshes and downstream modules can use the contact straight away.

Kernel stream

From grower delivery through cracking, drying, sorting, and packing — all the way to dispatch.

Grower Intake

This is where macadamia nuts arrive. When a grower delivers NIS (nut in shell), you log the delivery, run through a receiving checklist, capture samples, and create a kernel batch that production can pick up.

Work delivery by delivery. The flow is: receive → checklist → batch → sample/test → release. Nothing moves to production until the checklist is done and quality is satisfied.

How to use it

  1. Open Grower Intake from the sidebar under Kernel.
  2. Switch between Board (kanban-style) and Table view — use whichever your team prefers.
  3. Use Search and Status filters to find a specific batch.
  4. For each new delivery: start with the Receiving checklist — it's Stage 1 and unlocks everything else.
  5. After the checklist, run Batch test and Batch sample as your quality process requires.
  6. When quality is satisfied, use Release to production.
Tip: Use Export to get a spreadsheet snapshot — handy for audits or end-of-day reports.

Receiving checklist (grower)

This is Stage 1 of intake. Work through each line — weights, dockets, quality flags — exactly as your operations team defined. Everything else (batch test, sample, release) stays locked until this is done.

  1. Open from a batch card or row actions when the delivery is ready for checking.
  2. Complete every line item: weights, delivery docket, quality flags.
  3. If something's wrong with the delivery, record it here and escalate to QA before releasing forward.
  4. Save — the batch card updates to show the checklist as complete.

Create kernel batch (dialog)

Opens from Create kernel batch on the intake screen. Enter the grower (from CRM), delivery details, and wet NIS data. Save to create the batch — you'll return to the board to run checklist and tests.

Link sample to batch (dialog)

Links a lab sample result to an intake batch. Select the correct batch and sample identifiers — don't guess batch numbers. Save so quality status can progress.

End sample (dialog)

Records the final disposition of a sample. Note any retest requirements before saving.

End sample — view (dialog)

Read-only view of how a sample was closed. Open for reference during investigations.

Kernel Production

Once a batch is released from intake, it arrives here. Kernel production tracks each batch through its stages — receiving, cracking, drying, sorting, packing, and any QA steps your factory defines.

Drive each batch through its stages in order. The board shows you what's where. Job cards capture the detail. QA and stock hand-offs depend on accurate stage completion — so keep it truthful.

How to use it

  1. Open Kernel Production from the sidebar under Kernel.
  2. Search and filter batches. Toggle between Board, Table, or Approved jobcards views.
  3. Open a batch to manage its job card, advance production stages, and handle QA hand-offs.
  4. Complete stages in order so status stays accurate for everyone downstream.
  5. When finished, the batch moves to Stock (Kernel) automatically.

Kernel job card (dialog)

The job card captures production detail for a batch — styles, yields, equipment used. Fill it per your factory standard and save. Downstream release-to-stock steps depend on accurate job card data.

Job card — view (dialog)

Read-only view of a job card. Use it to verify styles and quantities before authorising dispatch or QA sign-off.

Production stages (dialog)

Advance or edit the production stages for a batch. Move stages in order — don't skip mandatory QA stages. Save after each meaningful change so the board reflects true floor status.

Production stages — view (dialog)

Read-only history of stage changes with timestamps. Useful when dispatch or QA asks where a batch stalled.

Stock Management

Stock management gives you a live view of your warehouse — both kernel and oil/protein, depending on which route you came from. You can see what's on hand, run stock takes, and send finished goods toward dispatch.

The key rule: only send to dispatch when physical stock matches the system. If something doesn't line up, fix it here first.

For kernel stock

  1. Open Stock (Kernel) from the sidebar under Kernel.
  2. Use By style, Weekly, or Overview tabs to see stock from different angles. In Adjust Stock, capture cartons or kg; the system calculates boxes before saving.
  3. Select finished kernel lines and click Send to Dispatch to build a dispatch basket.
  4. Use Import historical only for the controlled kernel load your admin approved.

For oil & protein stock

  1. Open Stock (Oil & Protein) from the sidebar under Oil & Protein.
  2. Add or import oil lots (from Excel if needed). Watch best-before dates and days remaining.
  3. Use Send to Dispatch to move released oil lots toward the oil dispatch module.
Tip: Use Export regularly for warehouse reports. Run stock takes through the app rather than ad-hoc spreadsheets — it keeps the audit trail clean.

Send to dispatch — kernel (dialog)

Opens from kernel stock when you select finished lines. Confirm the customer, delivery date, and quantities match what sales agreed. Submit and the basket appears in Kernel Dispatch for final inspection.

Send to dispatch — oil (dialog)

Same idea but for oil/protein lots. Verify lot numbers, best-before dates, and quantities against the physical warehouse before submitting.

Stock take (dialog)

Opens during a stock-take workflow. Count physically first, then enter counted quantities. Explain any variances in the notes field. Submit for approval if your workflow includes a reviewer.

Oil lot (dialog)

Create or edit an oil lot record. Enter best-before date, quantity, location, and grade. Make sure labels on physical drums or tanks match what you enter here.

Import oil lots (dialog)

Bulk-import oil lots from an Excel template. Don't rename columns in the template. Preview row counts before committing.

Raw material issued (dialog)

Records raw material issued from stock to production. Link to the correct batch or job — quantities reduce raw stock when saved.

Receiving checklist — stock (dialog)

Used for stock or supplier receiving checks. Complete quantities, condition checks, and sign-off fields. Save so stock and intake statuses can advance.

Kernel Dispatch

The final step in the kernel stream. Dispatch baskets are built from stock, inspected, and shipped to customers. Nothing leaves without paperwork.

The flow is: stock sends lines here → you build a basket → complete the inspection and dispatch form → mark as dispatched. If quantities don't match, fix it in Stock Management, not here.

How to use it

  1. Open Kernel Dispatch from the sidebar under Kernel.
  2. Use Board or Table view and hit Refresh to see the latest baskets.
  3. Open View sheet for basket lines and the full inspection and dispatch form.
  4. Click Dispatch to open the inspection and dispatch form.
  5. Complete vehicle details, seal numbers, weight checks, and sign-offs per your logistics SOP.
  6. Review Baskets marked as dispatched for completed paperwork and proof of shipment. If dispatch was recorded by mistake, use Edit on the card or table row (or open the sheet and choose Edit) to clear paperwork and move the basket back to ready to dispatch.

Kernel dispatch basket (dialog)

Create or edit a dispatch basket header — buyer, delivery date, references. Pick kernel customers from CRM. Save the header before opening line-level or inspection forms.

Kernel dispatch form (dialog)

The inspection and dispatch paperwork. Fill vehicle details, seal numbers, weight checks, and sign-offs. Submit only after the physical load matches the basket — no forcing through mismatches. After dispatch, the form is read-only unless you choose Edit to unlock the basket (clears saved paperwork) when dispatch was recorded in error.

Oil & protein stream

From raw ingredient delivery through oil and protein production to packed and dispatched product.

Supplier Intake

This is where raw ingredients arrive for the oil & protein stream — macadamia oil kernel, cracker dust, kernel dust, crush, cake, and similar materials from external suppliers.

The flow mirrors kernel intake but for oil ingredients: receive → checklist → batch → test → release. Each delivery creates one or more batches that move through sample testing before they're released to oil production.

How to use it

  1. Open Supplier Intake from the sidebar under Oil & Protein.
  2. Toggle between Board, Table, Weekly, and Overview views.
  3. Start a new delivery with Receiver checklist — this captures the delivery note, supplier, vehicle checks, and creates the batch.
  4. Once the batch appears on the board, complete sample tests (e.g. FFA) from the card or row actions.
  5. When testing is done and the batch is approved, it moves to Ready for oil production.
Tip: You can add a new supplier on the fly using the + button next to the supplier dropdown in the receiver checklist — no need to leave the page.

Supplier receiver checklist (dialog)

The starting point for oil-side deliveries. Capture the delivery note, pick the supplier from CRM, check vehicle and pallet condition, then add bag rows — each row can become its own batch with product type, batch number, and quantity (kg).

  1. Fill in the delivery note, date, and supplier.
  2. Complete vehicle and condition checks (clean truck, pests, pallets).
  3. Add one or more bag rows with product type, quantity, and optional dates.
  4. Submit — the batch appears on the board ready for sample testing.

Supplier oil batch (dialog)

Edit batch header details from the intake row actions. Some fields may be locked after testing is complete — only change what your role allows. Confirm status still matches physical stock before releasing to production.

Oil Production

Oil production is where raw ingredient batches become food-grade oil, cosmetic oil, or protein powder. You'll manage production sheets, oil bins, and shift data here.

Raw batches released from supplier intake appear in the Raw ingredients in production list. Run extraction, record production data, mark bags empty when done, and create production sheets for each product type.

How to use it

  1. Open Oil Production from the sidebar under Oil & Protein.
  2. Review Raw ingredients in production — these are batches released from Supplier Intake.
  3. Open a batch to run extraction steps. Mark a bag Empty when the press run is finished.
  4. Check Finished (emptied) raw batches to see what's already left the active list.
  5. Create a Production sheet (Food Grade Oil / Protein Powder / Cosmetic Oil) — choose form entry vs file upload if both are offered.
  6. Use View data and Refresh for analytics and to reload after others have changed data.

Oil production sheet (dialog)

Create or edit a production sheet. Choose Fill out the form or Upload a file if both modes exist. Enter yields, losses, and product splits. Attach lab results if needed. Save so finished goods and stock reflect the run.

Oil & Protein Dispatch

The mirror of kernel dispatch, but for oil and protein products. Build baskets from released oil lots, run inspections, complete dispatch forms, and ship.

Same pattern as kernel dispatch: basket → checks → ship. Make sure oil lots are released and available in stock before adding them to a load.

How to use it

  1. Open Oil & Protein Dispatch from the sidebar.
  2. Use Board or Table view. Hit Refresh to see the latest baskets.
  3. Build or review baskets from released oil lots.
  4. Complete the inspection and dispatch form before the truck leaves.
  5. Only load lots that are released and available in stock.

Oil dispatch basket (dialog)

Create or edit a dispatch basket header — customer, delivery window, references. Pick oil & protein customers from CRM. Confirm only released lots are referenced. Save the header before opening inspection or dispatch forms.

Oil dispatch form (dialog)

Inspection and despatch paperwork for oil/protein. Complete checks, transport details, and signatures. Submit when the load is confirmed.

Quality & traceability

Recording tests, tracking batches across the system, and keeping the audit trail clean.

Quality Assurance

Quality is where you record tests — moisture, FFA, peroxide, organoleptic, and whatever else your food-safety programme requires. Failed or out-of-spec results can block the next step, depending on how your site is configured.

Log tests against the right batch or item. Follow your local QA playbook for pass/fail thresholds — the system keeps a trail of who did what and when.

How to use it

  1. Open Quality Assurance from the sidebar.
  2. Use Search, Test type, and Result filters to find the record you need.
  3. Click New quality test to log a new sample, batch, or final-product test.
  4. Open a row to enter results, attach evidence files, or sign off.
  5. Failed or conditional results may block release — follow your QA rules.
  6. Use Export when auditors or customers need evidence outside the app.

Quality test (dialog)

Log or edit a specific test result. Choose pass/fail/conditional and attach evidence files if required. Save — failed tests may block release depending on your configuration.

Batch Journey

Batch Journey is your cross-cutting search tool. If you know a batch number (or a grower, or a status) but not which module currently owns the work, start here. It shows every batch across intake, production, QA, and dispatch in one view.

Think of it as a universal search for batches. Find the batch, read its status, then click through to the owning module to take the next action.

How to use it

  1. Open Batch Journey from the sidebar.
  2. Type in the search field — batch number, grower name, etc.
  3. Set Status and Sort (newest, by status, by grower, weight, moisture) to narrow the list.
  4. Read the Status column: Intake, Receiving, Production, QA, Dispatch, or Complete.
  5. Click the batch number link to open detail or jump into the owning module.

Batch history (dialog)

Read-only detail for one batch, split across Dispatch, Intake, Production, and End sample (QA) tabs. Dispatch lists orders and per-style cartons and kg for this batch; other tabs hold the timeline (checklist, samples, production days, job card, QA). Empty tabs are hidden. Use during investigations.

Batch summary (dialog)

Consolidated batch metadata — grower, weights, moisture, status — in one view. Use it to confirm details before answering customer queries or signing off.

Bulk-import data from Excel into Macavation — contacts, stock items, oil production sheets, and more. Download the template first, fill it without changing column headers, then upload and preview before committing.

Tips & support

A few habits that will save you time and headaches.

Good habits

  1. Save before closing. Watch for the on-screen success message before moving on.
  2. Refresh after changes. If a dropdown looks out of date (e.g. after adding a CRM contact), refresh or reopen the page.
  3. Use consistent batch numbering. Agree on naming conventions with your team — kernel and oil rules may differ.
  4. Don't force dispatch. If quantities don't match, fix it in Stock Management. Forcing mismatches through dispatch creates downstream problems.
  5. Keep test data separate. Never run test scenarios or import test data on a production database.

Browsers

Use a current version of Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. If the app behaves oddly after an update, clear your browser cache or try a private/incognito window.
