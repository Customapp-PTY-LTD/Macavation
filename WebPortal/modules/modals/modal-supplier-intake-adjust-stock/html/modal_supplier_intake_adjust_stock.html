Creates supplier intake batches without the receiving checklist. Supplier name, each row’s batch number, and manufactured date are required. Best-before is calculated automatically as 18 months after manufactured date. If you enter FFA (%) for a row, that batch is saved as Ready for Oil Production; if FFA is left blank, it stays in Awaiting tests until you record FFA via Sample test.
