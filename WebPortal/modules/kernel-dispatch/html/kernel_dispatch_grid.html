1 · Dispatch created
Pete or Mike uses Send to Dispatch. A "Pick a dispatch" task opens for the Production Manager.
2 · Stock picked
Thando confirms what she picked, with substitutes if a line is short. Changes are sent to Joslyn, Mike and Pete.
3 · Pricing confirmed
Prices fill in from the customer’s price list. Pete can change any price, then confirms. The sale is recorded.
4 · Truck loaded
When the truck arrives: truck details and checks. Only after pricing is confirmed.
5 · Invoice sent
Joslyn gets a task to send the invoice and documents, and ticks it off.
