Supply Chain & Production Process Flow

Help Grower Intake Stock Oil Production

Grower/supplier intake → warehouse raws → production → finished product → customers & debtors. Document types: GRV (Goods Receiving Voucher), IBT (Internal Transfer), INV (Inventory).

Implementation Checklist
  • Grower Intake: Screen + Quality Check Receiving Doc; IBT to W/House Raws (NIS = R NIL) and to PROCESSED. Open
  • Supplier Intake: Screen + GRV/INV to W/House Raws (OIL KERNEL R YES).
  • W/House Raws: Two stock types (NIS = R NIL, OIL KERNEL R YES); IBT out to PRODUCTION. Open
  • PROCESSED: Link to BATCH # / Work Ticket; flow to USK. Open
  • USK: GRV to W/House Finished (KERNEL R YES); INV to KERNEL CUSTOMERS and DEBTORS.
  • PRODUCTION: Input from W/House Raws (IBT); outputs Oil Cosmetic, Oil Food, Protein, Feed; GRV to W/House Finished (OIL PROTEIN R YES). Open
  • W/House Finished Product: Two sections (KERNEL R YES, OIL PROTEIN R YES); INV to respective customer streams and DEBTORS. Open
  • Customers & Debtors: Kernel vs Feed/Oil/Protein customer types and debtor linkage. Open
